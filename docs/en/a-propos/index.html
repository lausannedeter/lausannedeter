Lausanne Deter

Who?

We are Lausanne Deter, a collective whose goal is to make visible the different activist events taking place in the Lausanne region. We aim to highlight all struggles positioned on the left side of the political spectrum and fighting against all forms of oppression.

We do not align ourselves with one specific ideological stance and believe that what matters most is building convergence between struggles. By making our various events visible, we become stronger together, because it is through numbers that we will put an end to systemic oppression.

Why?

We noticed that it can sometimes be difficult to stay informed about the different events taking place in the region. Through this calendar, we want to highlight these events and make them as accessible as possible.

We believe that making activism more accessible, especially through this tool, will help our movement grow stronger and carry more weight in the ongoing material and ideological struggle.

How?

Each month, activist events are listed on this website. If you are a collective or venue and would like to make one or more events visible, send us an email at lausannedeter@proton.me and find us on Instagram at @lsn.dtr.

You can print our calendars and display them in your various spaces so you always have the month’s program in sight :)
